\# Business Problem



ClassicModels is a global retailer of scale model cars.



The management wants to analyze historical sales data to answer important business questions.



The objectives are:



\- Analyze sales performance.

\- Identify top customers.

\- Identify top-selling products.

\- Measure employee performance.

\- Compare office performance.

\- Generate business insights and recommendations.



This project simulates the work of a Data Analyst in a real business environment.
